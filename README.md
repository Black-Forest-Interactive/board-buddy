# Board Buddy

**Board Buddy** is a digital companion app for [Sid Meier's Civilization: The Board Game (2010)](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/77130/sid-meiers-civilization-the-board-game). It brings the combat and research system to your phone or browser, so you and your fellow players can manage battles, track technologies, and build armies without having to shuffle through stacks of cards at the table.

> **Try it now:** [blackforrestdevelopment.de](https://blackforrestdevelopment.de)

---

## What it does

- **Sessions** — one player creates a game session, others join via a share code or QR code
- **Nations** — each player picks a civilization to represent them throughout the game
- **Army management** — produce and track units (Infantry, Artillery, Cavalry, Planes, …) with their individual health and damage values
- **Technology research** — unlock techs tier by tier; each discovery upgrades your unit types
- **Combat** — resolve battles digitally: deploy units to fronts, take turns attacking, track HP in real time, and get a clear winner at the end
